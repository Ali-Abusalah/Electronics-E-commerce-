About DCTech Shop

DCTech Shop started in 2019 with a simple idea: buying electronics should be easy, honest and a little bit fun. Today we serve more than 120,000 customers with TVs, smartphones, laptops and accessories — hand-picked by our team and backed by a 2-year warranty on everything we sell.

What we stand for

  • Honest pricing — no fake discounts or hidden fees, ever.
  • Products we’d buy ourselves — every item is tested by our team before it hits the shelves.
  • Support that answers — real humans on chat and phone, 24/7.
  • Giving back — 1% of every order goes to local STEM programs.

Good to know

Returns & Refunds

Changed your mind? No problem. You have 30 days from delivery to return any item in its original condition for a full refund.

How it works

  1. Request a return — email alissalah@gmail.com or reply to your order confirmation with your order number.
  2. Pack it up — include all accessories and the original packaging.
  3. Ship it back — we email you a free prepaid return label.
  4. Get refunded — refunds land in 5–7 business days on your original payment method.

Conditions

  • Items must be unused and in their original packaging.
  • Defective or damaged items are replaced or refunded in full — no questions asked.
  • Clearance items and opened software can only be exchanged.
  • Return shipping is free on all standard returns.

Questions? Our support team is available 24/7 at +962 7 9880 9635.
